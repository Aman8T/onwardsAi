Senior Growth Marketing Manager
Lumen Retail · Mumbai, India · Hybrid (3 days a week in the office)

About Lumen Retail
Lumen Retail runs 140 home-lighting stores and a fast-growing online shop. We are building a growth team that treats every channel as an experiment.

What you will do
- Own acquisition and retention across paid, lifecycle and owned channels for the online shop.
- Run a disciplined experimentation programme: hypotheses, holdouts and readouts.
- Build the weekly performance view for the leadership team.
- Lead a team of marketers and agency partners.

What you need
- Must have 5+ years of growth, performance or lifecycle marketing experience at a consumer brand.
- Must have hands-on ownership of a paid media budget above ₹1 crore a year across Meta Ads and Google Ads.
- Required: strong command of lifecycle marketing on email and WhatsApp, using an ESP such as Klaviyo, CleverTap or MoEngage.
- Required: a proven A/B testing and experimentation practice.
- Required: experience leading a team of at least 5 people.
- Required: fluent in English and Hindi.
- Must have worked with a customer data platform such as Segment or mParticle.

Nice to have
- Experience building dashboards in Looker Studio or Power BI.
- Marketing mix modelling or multi-touch attribution.
- Shopify Plus experience.
- MBA or postgraduate degree in marketing.
- Familiarity with SQL.

Eligibility
- Must be authorised to work in India and able to be in the Mumbai office three days a week.
- Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree.
